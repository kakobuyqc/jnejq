The Litbuy shopping guide

Three passes. A clearer shortlist.

Keep your Litbuy product list useful by recording comparable details rather than saving links without context.

01 / DISCOVER

Start with a specific need

Choose an item type, preferred fit, color, and budget. A Litbuy clothing spreadsheet becomes easier to use when tops, outerwear, bottoms, and accessories have their own categories.

02 / COMPARE

Look beyond the first photo

For Litbuy finds, compare garment measurements, listed materials, close-up images, and seller information. In a Litbuy shoe spreadsheet, record the sizing system and any available foot-length guidance.

03 / PLAN

Record the full cost picture

Save the item price, currency, and date checked. Add separate estimates for delivery, service fees, and applicable taxes. Recheck the live listing before making a purchase.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds. Useful fields include the product link, category, size measurements, listed price, materials, photo notes, and estimated additional costs. This page is an independent guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Litbuy service.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with an item you already own. For tops, record chest width, shoulder width, and length. For bottoms, compare waist, rise, inseam, and thigh measurements. Confirm the measurement units.

Does a spreadsheet verify a seller or product?

No. A spreadsheet organizes information but does not verify authenticity, seller reliability, stock, or product quality. Review the individual listing, seller terms, available photos, and purchase protections before ordering.

What costs belong in a shopping shortlist?

Record the listed product price and separately estimate domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.
